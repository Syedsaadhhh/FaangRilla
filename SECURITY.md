# Security and Privacy Policy

## OpenDoor Relay Security Contract

OpenDoor Relay coordinates accessibility accommodations for community events. Handling access requests requires strict privacy and security boundaries.

### Privacy and Data Minimization Boundaries

1. **Functional Access Needs Only:**
   - The system strictly records functional accommodations (e.g., CART captioning, ASL interpretation, wheelchair accessible seating, assistive listening device).
   - The system **never** collects, infers, stores, or transmits medical diagnoses, disability classifications, or health records.
2. **Deterministic Consent and Disclosure Scope:**
   - Disclosures to providers are limited strictly to what is required to deliver the accommodation (e.g., language, technical format, venue equipment).
   - Attendee real identities are aliased or minimized unless explicit attendee consent is given.
3. **Expiring, Hashed Provider Tokens:**
   - Provider response links use cryptographically secure random tokens.
   - Tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes in internal state and expire automatically after the case deadline.
